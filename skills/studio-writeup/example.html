What changed in the crumb study

Three hydration levels, one flour, the same bake. Wetter dough opened the crumb, but past 78% the loaf lost its height.

The setup

Each loaf used 500 g of the same bread flour, 2% salt and 20% levain, shaped the same way and baked covered for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 25.

The water

Past 78%, the dough has more water than this flour can hold, so it spreads before it rises.

The shaping

A wetter dough needs a tighter shape to hold its height; the same shaping was used for all three.

What to try next

  • Hold 74% and change only the bulk time.
  • Repeat 78% with a stronger flour, to see whether the loss of height is the flour or the water.
